KEY METRICS

Walk-Forward Efficiency (WFE):
- Measures generalization: OOS Sharpe / IS Sharpe
- > 0.70 = EXCELLENT (minimal overfitting)
- 0.50-0.70 = GOOD (acceptable degradation)
- 0.30-0.50 = MARGINAL (significant overfitting)
- < 0.30 = POOR (severe overfitting)

Parameter Grid:
- Fast (default): 162 combinations, 2-5 minutes
- Full (optional): 2,500 combinations, 10-30 minutes

Rolling Windows:
- Default: 6 months training, 2-3 months testing
- Non-overlapping test periods for true OOS validation
- Supports custom window sizes
