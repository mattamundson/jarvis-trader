# JARVIS Morning Alpha — Edge Research & Relative Strength Sprint Design

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Status:** Approved design  
**Scope:** Research Sprint 1 for `relative_strength_breakout@1.0.0`, plus reusable edge-decomposition infrastructure for later families.

## 1. Objective

Determine whether Morning Alpha contains **incremental, persistent trading edge** rather than merely market beta, sector beta, or favorable sample-period drift. The first sprint isolates the Relative Strength Breakout family and measures five distinct sources of value:

1. **Selection edge** — choosing the stock adds value versus matched controls.
2. **Timing edge** — the entry trigger improves outcomes versus simpler entry timing.
3. **Exit edge** — stop/target logic captures more useful excursion than naive exits.
4. **Portfolio/filter edge** — sector/regime/heat gates improve risk-adjusted outcomes.
5. **Execution survival** — any advantage remains after realistic costs/slippage/latency stress.

The goal is not to maximize backtest P&L. The goal is to **prove or falsify incremental edge with transparent counterfactuals and honest trial accounting**.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Research Rules

- Use only certified point-in-time historical data that passes the existing Morning Alpha research readiness gate.
- Freeze `relative_strength_breakout@1.0.0`; no v1 rule changes after final OOS observation.
- Preserve failed tests and trial counts.
- Controls must be selected using information available at the original decision timestamp.
- Random controls must be deterministic under a declared seed.
- No control may sample from today's survivor universe when testing historical dates.
- Forward returns, MFE and MAE must never read beyond the requested horizon.
- Adding future rows must not change an earlier research observation.
- No ML is used in Sprint 1.
- No automatic optimizer selects a production rule.
- Gate-v2 remains the repository's canonical promotion authority; new research metrics do not replace it.

## 3. Sprint 1 Population

### 3.1 Strategy

`relative_strength_breakout@1.0.0`

### 3.2 Candidate population

Candidates must be generated by the existing deterministic detector from the same certified point-in-time universe used by live/backtest parity code.

A candidate record must preserve at minimum:

- symbol;
- decision timestamp;
- sector as known at the decision timestamp;
- strategy version;
- setup feature snapshot;
- candidate entry/stop;
- market regime;
- sector rank/context;
- provider/data-lineage identifiers.

## 4. Control Groups

Every Morning Alpha candidate is paired with controls that share the original decision timestamp.

### 4.1 Random eligible-universe control

Select one symbol from the point-in-time eligible universe using a deterministic seed derived from campaign ID + strategy version + candidate identity.

### 4.2 Random same-sector control

Select one symbol from the same historical sector and eligible universe using the same deterministic-seed discipline.

If no valid same-sector control exists, the observation is marked unavailable; it is never backfilled from a future universe.

### 4.3 Same-stock next-open control

Use the same Morning Alpha symbol but enter using the next eligible regular-session open instead of the strategy trigger. This isolates trigger/timing value from selection value.

### 4.4 SPY control

Measure the matching holding-window return for SPY from the same timestamp/horizon.

### 4.5 Sector ETF control

Measure the corresponding sector ETF over the same timestamp/horizon, respecting historical ETF inception. A sector ETF that did not exist at the historical timestamp is unavailable unless a separately approved PIT proxy specification exists.

## 5. New Research Modules

Create bounded research components under `engine/morning_alpha/research/`.

### 5.1 `controls.py`

Responsibilities:

- define `ControlKind`;
- define immutable `ControlObservation` records;
- perform deterministic point-in-time control selection;
- reject future/ineligible controls;
- report unavailable controls explicitly.

### 5.2 `forward_returns.py`

Responsibilities:

- calculate candidate/control returns at fixed horizons;
- calculate benchmark-relative returns;
- enforce anti-lookahead boundaries;
- retain exact start/end timestamps and pricing basis.

Pre-registered horizons:

- 30 minutes;
- 60 minutes;
- end of session;
- 1 trading day;
- 2 trading days;
- 3 trading days;
- 5 trading days;
- 10 trading days;
- 20 trading days.

### 5.3 `attribution.py`

Provide transparent counterfactual attribution. Sprint 1 does **not** use ML/Shapley attribution.

Initial components:

- broad-market contribution;
- sector contribution;
- stock-selection increment;
- timing increment;
- exit increment when exit studies are supplied;
- filter/portfolio increment when ablation studies are supplied;
- residual interaction term.

Attribution must mathematically reconcile to the observed strategy-minus-baseline difference within numerical tolerance.

### 5.4 `ablation.py`

Support one-component-at-a-time diagnostic variants without mutating the original strategy version.

Initial labels:

- `FULL`;
- `FULL_MINUS_SECTOR_FILTER`;
- `FULL_MINUS_VOLUME_CONFIRMATION`;
- `FULL_MINUS_REGIME_FILTER`;
- `FULL_MINUS_ENTRY_CONFIRMATION`.

Ablations are research variants only. A successful ablation does not silently replace v1; a new strategy version is required for later promotion.

### 5.5 `trial_ledger.py`

Record every experiment attempted, including failed/unfavorable trials.

Required fields:

- immutable trial ID;
- campaign ID;
- strategy version;
- control version;
- trigger version;
- exit version;
- parameter set / ablation label;
- cost assumption;
- data-manifest/hash identity;
- sample interval;
- sample size;
- status;
- output artifact hash/location where applicable.

The ledger supplies honest trial counts for DSR/Gate-v2 and later PBO/CSCV analysis.

## 6. Forward-Return and Excursion Study

Before trigger or exit optimization, estimate raw alpha decay.

For each candidate/control measure forward returns across all pre-registered horizons.

Also expand research excursion curves:

### MFE horizons

- 30m;
- 60m;
- EOD;
- 1d;
- 2d;
- 3d;
- 5d.

### MAE horizons

- 30m;
- 60m;
- EOD;
- 1d;
- 2d;
- 3d;
- 5d.

Derived probabilities include:

- P(MFE >= 1R/2R/3R/4R);
- P(MAE <= -0.5R/-1R);
- median time to 1R;
- median time to 2R.

This determines whether current 2R-4R targets and stops are structurally plausible before exit optimization begins.

## 7. Trigger Comparison

Use the existing frozen trigger set:

- market open;
- prior-day high;
- premarket high;
- 5m ORB;
- 15m ORB;
- 30m ORB;
- breakout/retest;
- VWAP reclaim.

For every trigger report:

- trigger rate;
- expectancy R;
- median R;
- win rate;
- profit factor;
- MFE/MAE;
- filtered-loser rate;
- filtered-winner rate;
- time to trigger;
- cost/slippage sensitivity.

## 8. Exit Laboratory

Test exits independently against the same frozen entry population.

Initial variants:

- stop + 1R;
- stop + 2R;
- stop + 3R;
- stop + 4R;
- 50% at 1R / 50% at 2R;
- 50% at 1R / 25% at 2R / 25% trail;
- ATR trail;
- end-of-day;
- 1-day hold;
- 3-day hold;
- 5-day hold.

No exit is promoted solely because it is the in-sample best performer.

## 9. Robustness / Parameter Stability

Parameter research must prefer broad stable plateaus over point optima.

For each tested parameter grid compute:

- local-neighborhood expectancy;
- rank stability;
- sensitivity to adjacent parameter values;
- OOS degradation;
- sample size by cell.

A narrow isolated optimum is treated as fragile evidence.

## 10. Statistical Controls

Sprint 1 records every trial. Existing Deflated Sharpe/Gate-v2 remain mandatory.

Later research may add PBO/CSCV and SPA/Reality-Check methods, but Sprint 1 must make those additions possible by preserving the complete trial ledger and variant identities.

## 11. Cross-Validation Plan

Required evaluations:

1. chronological development/validation/OOS split;
2. rolling walk-forward;
3. sector holdouts where sample sizes permit;
4. symbol holdouts where sample sizes permit;
5. regime-segment results;
6. cost/slippage stress.

No final OOS block is reused for parameter selection.

## 12. Regime Conditioning

At minimum segment by:

- SPY above/below 200DMA;
- SPY intermediate trend direction;
- VIX buckets (<15, 15-20, 20-30, >30);
- breadth strong/neutral/weak;
- rates rising/falling;
- QQQ/IWM leadership;
- major scheduled event categories when certified data exists.

Regime findings are diagnostic first. Turning a regime finding into a production filter requires a new version and another validation cycle.

## 13. Execution Stress

At minimum stress:

- 0/2/5/10/20 bps round-trip cost assumptions consistent with existing research conventions;
- entry slippage shocks expressed as ATR fractions;
- delayed-entry scenarios where intraday data permits.

Any edge that disappears under modest realistic degradation is rejected.

## 14. Relative Strength Promotion Test

`relative_strength_breakout@1.0.0` cannot proceed merely because absolute P&L is positive.

It must demonstrate incremental value versus the applicable controls and satisfy the existing Morning Alpha/Gate-v2 research rules, including at minimum:

- >=200 historical triggered observations for normal-path promotion;
- positive net expectancy after realistic costs;
- positive chronological OOS evidence;
- acceptable walk-forward consistency;
- acceptable cost/slippage survival;
- no single tiny symbol subset or single regime explaining nearly all profit;
- reasonable parameter-neighborhood stability;
- canonical Gate-v2 PASS before paper observation.

## 15. Output Artifact

Sprint 1 generates a versioned research report containing:

- candidate/control sample counts;
- forward-return curves;
- incremental selection alpha;
- trigger comparison;
- MFE/MAE curves;
- exit comparison;
- ablation results;
- regime table;
- cost/slippage results;
- OOS/WFO results;
- Monte Carlo summary where applicable;
- trial count;
- Gate-v2 verdict;
- explicit PASS/FAIL/INSUFFICIENT-DATA conclusion.

## 16. Failure Semantics

The sprint must fail closed when:

- certified data is unavailable;
- control populations are materially unavailable;
- future-data leakage is detected;
- sample size falls below the pre-registered requirement for promotion;
- attribution does not reconcile;
- output artifacts cannot be bound to the research-data identity.

A research failure is preserved as evidence; it is not silently retried with altered parameters under the same version.

## 17. Definition of Done

This design is implemented when:

- controls are PIT-safe and deterministic;
- forward-return horizons are anti-lookahead tested;
- attribution reconciles;
- ablations are isolated and immutable;
- trial ledger preserves every experiment identity;
- MFE/MAE horizon curves are supported;
- Relative Strength Sprint 1 can execute from certified data and emit a versioned report;
- the complete existing Morning Alpha regression suite still passes;
- no live-order capability is introduced.
