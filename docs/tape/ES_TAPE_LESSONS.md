2026-10-27 | volume pace on legs | Volume pace means comparing how much volume each leg of the day trades per bar, because a move on fading volume is running out of fuel and a move on expanding volume has real participation. Today (IB ESZ26 5m) the 08:30-09:25 drop averaged about 20.7k contracts a bar but stalled at 7712.50 (09:20), and the 09:30 bar that reversed it printed 34.0k (the biggest RTH bar before the close) on a +8 point close at 7735.75. The 09:30-11:00 rally kept pace at about 18.3k a bar up to 7772.00 (11:00), then midday balance dried up to about 7.0k a bar (11:55-13:55) inside 7750.75-7760.75. The 14:00-14:45 push back to 7772.75 (14:45) came on only about 12.0k a bar and failed at the same high, then the 14:50 (34.0k) and 14:55 (94.1k) MOC bars sold it to 7757.00. Next time, when a retest of the high comes on roughly two-thirds the volume of the leg that made it, treat it as a likely double top, not a breakout, and wait for volume expansion before trusting a new high.
2026-10-28 | failed breakout and trapped buyers | A failed breakout is a push through a known level that can't hold, which leaves the traders who bought the break trapped above and turns their stops into fuel the other way. Today (IB ESZ26) the 11:54 1-minute bar ran 7774.25 to 7787.25 on 21.5k contracts, through ONH 7778.25, but the very next minute closed 7779.00 and the 5m bars faded to 7766.75 by 12:25 as those buyers exited back near VWAP. The 13:00 FOMC bar did the same thing faster, spiking to 7788.50 and closing 7769.50 in the same minute. Next time, judge a break by where the next one or two bars close, not by how big the spike was: a giant-volume push that closes back inside the old range within a bar or two is a trap, and the old level becomes the line the trapped side has to fight from.

2026-10-29 | MOC imbalance as one-shot flow | A market-on-close imbalance is a one-time, price-insensitive order that has to fill at the 15:00 print, so it can push price into the close but leaves nobody behind to defend that price afterward. Today the 14:50 CT MOC showed an S&P +$4.48B BUY, and ES rose from a 14:45 close of 7743.75 to 7749.75 (14:50, 34.9k) and 7754.25 (14:55 bar, 82.0k contracts, H 7756.0). The moment the closing auction was done, the 15:00 bar opened 7754.0 and fell to 7723.25 (45.7k), straight through the 7736 put wall, and reached 7712.25 by 15:10, with ZeroGEX still about -20B short gamma under a 7779 flip. Next time, treat a MOC-driven ramp as borrowed price: it can carry a position to the bell, but in short gamma do not read it as new demand that will hold after 15:00.
2026-10-30 | VWAP as the trend-day line | On a trend day, session VWAP (the volume-weighted average price since the 08:30 open) works as the line the trend side defends, so bounces into it are where the trend resumes rather than where it reverses. Today (IB ESZ6 5m) the 09:35-09:55 bounce got back above VWAP (closes 7655.0-7653.75 against a VWAP near 7651) but never built on it, and the 10:00 bar closed 7645.0 back under. From then on no bar even touched VWAP for almost five hours while price stepped down through the 11:00-11:40 balance (7622-7636), breaking it at 11:45 on 36.3k contracts, about double the ~14k bars before it, and on to 7558.25 at 13:50. The only test came from the 14:50 MOC buy flow (H 7620.5 vs VWAP about 7617.7), and that bar still closed under at 7614.75. Next time, once a down day closes back below VWAP after a failed reclaim, treat every rally into VWAP as a place sellers show up, and wait for two 5m closes above it before calling a bottom.
2026-11-02 | initial balance range extension | The initial balance is the first hour's range (08:30-09:25 CT), and when price later closes outside it on rising volume, that range extension shows which side the bigger, slower money has picked for the day. Today (Yahoo ES=F 5m, IB down) the IB was a wide 7641.50-7693.75, and the high was set inside it at 09:25; the 09:30 bar couldn't extend it (H 7691.50) and the 09:30-11:20 bars drifted back down on only about 14.0k contracts a bar. The downside extension came at 11:30/11:35, two 5m closes under the 7641.50 IB low (7638.50, 7637.00), and the 11:45 and 11:50 bars expanded to 29.3k and 34.7k, roughly double the midday pace. From there price never got back inside the IB and stretched to 7570.75 at 14:55, about 71 points or 1.35 IB ranges below the low. Next time, when a wide IB fails to extend on its own side and then closes outside the other side on expanding volume, treat it as the day's direction and stop looking for a return to the IB midpoint (7667.62 today).
2026-11-03 | single prints as unfinished business | Single prints are prices the market ran through so fast on a prior day that only one 30-minute period traded there, and they act as a magnet because the auction left that area untested. Monday's selloff left selling single prints up to 7623, and today (IB ESZ6 5m) price worked back up through them in steps: 7616.25 at 09:55, 7618.25 at 10:15, then 7623.75 at 12:30 on 13.5k contracts, filling them to the tick. Once the gap was repaired there was nothing left to pull price higher; the 12:35 bar closed 7617.0, two closes under 7612 followed at 13:05/13:10, and ES slid to 7587.25 by 13:55. Next time, treat a prior day's single prints as a target the market will often come back to finish, and once they are filled, look for responsive sellers there rather than a breakout.
2026-11-04 | long gamma compresses range | When dealers flip from short gamma to long gamma, their hedging switches from chasing price to leaning against it, so a fast trend morning tends to turn into a tight, pinned afternoon near the biggest strike. Today ZeroGEX went from about -34.6B short gamma (11/3 14:59 map) to +13.19B long by 10:59 CT, with the flip near 7709 and the pin at 7731-7741, after ES ran from 7679.75 (08:30) to 7731.5 (08:40) (IB ESZ6 5m). From there the average 5m bar shrank from about 15.2 points in the first hour to about 6.4 points from 11:00 to 14:45, and 53 of the 75 closes after 08:45 sat inside 7725-7741 while the HOD was only 7746.25 (12:05). Next time, once price is above the flip in long gamma, expect targets beyond the pin to stall (DoubleWide's 7759 missed by 12.75) and treat breaks of the range as fade candidates rather than new trend legs.

2026-11-05 | gap-through with no retest | A gap-through is when the open drives straight through a key overnight level and never comes back to let the other side exit or a limit entry fill, which confirms initiative and leaves planned passive entries stranded. Today (IB ESZ6 5m) ONL was 7691; the 08:30 bar already closed 7674.50 on 73.3k contracts and the 08:35 bar closed 7659.00 on 63.4k, so the short trigger fired, but the high of that confirming bar was only 7679.50 and no later RTH bar traded back to the ~7689 plan entry. By 08:40 the low was 7621.25 (T2 7639 already through) on 76.1k, and the session never looked back, finishing 7370.25 with LOD 7365.25 at 15:00. Next time, once two 5m closes print through an overnight extreme and the next bar's high stays well inside the break, treat the level as abandoned: do not wait for a retest fill, and size or skip under the strict-fill rule instead of chasing.

2026-11-09 | open-test-drive | An open-test-drive is an open where price first probes one side to see if anyone wants to trade there, finds nothing, then reverses and drives the other way without coming back to the open. Today (IB ESZ6 5m) ES opened 7465.25 and the 08:30 bar tested down to 7455.75, into the 7452-7460 band both newsletters flagged, yet still closed 7479.50, above the 7476.50 overnight high, on 47.9k contracts. The 08:35 bar (close 7495.50, 41.7k) and 08:40 bar (close 7512.50, 35.6k) followed, price never traded below 7493.00 again, and the high came at 7600.50 (14:15). The failed test showed sellers were absent where they should have been strongest, which made the drive the higher-odds side. Next time, when the first 5m bar tests a known level, rejects it, and closes beyond the overnight extreme on heavy volume, call it an open-test-drive and don't wait for a pullback to the open to get involved.

2026-11-10 | poor low (flat low, no excess) | A poor low is a session low built by several bars stalling at nearly the same price with no sharp rejection tail, which says the selling simply ran out rather than buyers stepping in hard. Today from 11:35 to 12:25 CT ten straight 5-minute bars put their lows between 7493.00 and 7503.00 (LOD 7493.00 on the 11:40 bar), and price only lifted off at 12:30 (close 7515.50). Because no excess tail formed, profile traders treat 7493 as unfinished business that markets often come back to retest. The practical point: a flat low is a magnet below, not firm support to lean a swing long on. (IB ESZ6 5m, bar start times)
